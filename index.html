Who we supply

Three kinds of buyers, each with its own pack sizes, paperwork and delivery rhythm.

  • Food factories

    Drums in brine and IQF cartons with a fixed cut and size, so your line runs the same every week. Spec sheet and lab analysis with each lot.

  • Hotels, restaurants and caterers

    3 kg tins, pails and pouches that open ready to use. Less draining, less waste and a portion cost you can plan with.

  • Importers and wholesalers

    Full or mixed containers, retail jars under your own label on request, and export documents prepared for customs clearance.

How an order runs

The same five steps for every order, from the first email to unloading.

  1. Inquiry

    Tell us the product, cut, pack, quantity and where it should go.

  2. Spec and samples

    We send the specification sheet and, if you want, samples from the producing plant.

  3. Offer

    Price per pack or per kg, FOB Egypt or delivered, with lead time and payment terms.

  4. Production and checks

    Packed to your spec. Each lot is checked before loading; frozen goods stay at −18 °C.

  5. Shipping and documents

    Sea freight in dry or reefer containers. Documents reach you and your forwarder before the goods do.
