Why Egypt

Egypt grows artichokes, peppers, olives, okra and strawberries at scale, and its main harvests fall in the cooler months, when fields in Europe are quiet. Processing plants close to the farms grill, brine, pack and freeze soon after harvest.

Goods of Egyptian origin can enter the EU with an EUR.1 certificate under the EU–Egypt Association Agreement, which for many products means reduced or zero customs duty.

How we choose producers

  • We visit plants before we offer their products, and look at processing, storage and hygiene ourselves.
  • Certificates held by the producing plant, for example BRCGS, IFS, FSSC 22000, Halal or Kosher, come with the offer.
  • Each product has one spec sheet: cut, size, drained weight, salt or acidity and microbiological limits, agreed before production.
  • On request, you get samples from the production lot before you commit.

Checks on every lot

  • Drained weight and fill checked against the spec sheet.
  • Salt, acidity and pH measured for products in brine.
  • Metal detection on the packing lines.
  • Temperature records for frozen goods, from cold store to container.
  • Retained samples kept from every lot.
  • Pre-shipment inspection by an independent surveyor, if you ask for it.

Documents with each shipment

  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Certificate of origin, and EUR.1 for EU destinations
  • Health or phytosanitary certificate where required
  • Certificate of analysis for the lot
  • Bill of lading
  • Halal or Kosher certificates on request
